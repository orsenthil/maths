A two-player game-theory puzzle

Take the Ends

Coins sit in a row, each worth a different amount. On your turn, take the coin from the left end or the right end — never the middle — and keep it. Play until the row is empty, then see who collected more.

Game from Alexander Bogomolny's Cut the Knot, after a puzzle in Peter Winkler's Mathematical Puzzles: A Connoisseur's Collection (A K Peters, 2004).

How to play

Coins are laid out in a fixed row. Players alternate turns; each turn, take the coin at either end of what's left and add it to your pile — the middle is off limits. Play continues until no coins remain, then whoever collected the larger total wins. Equal totals are a draw.
